Four ways to hold the controls.

  1. APanelInter-lab style. Text fills the screen, one dense ruled panel on the right. Bottom sheet with group tabs on mobile.
  2. BToolbarNothing but text until you ask. A black floating bar of groups; each opens a ruled sheet above it.
  3. CLedgerThe site's label gutter. Every control visible at once as a ruled sheet; a black strip of values follows you down.
  4. DScrubNo sliders. Parameters read as lines of values you drag sideways; the column's edge sets its width.
