    settings ↓built from S05, S02, S11 and T01

    Momentum: after you let go of a drag, the value keeps moving and slows down. Short stops in about 0.15 s, long in about 0.35 s, the way a phone list coasts. With momentum on, a wheel notch also glides to its next step instead of jumping. Trackpads already send their own momentum, so they feel the same in every mode. Vertical wheel. Off: the wheel always scrolls the page. Focus: only the control you clicked or tabbed to. Hover: once the pointer has rested on a control for 0.4 s and the page has stopped moving. Always: any control under the pointer. A scroll that started on the page keeps scrolling the page in every mode.
