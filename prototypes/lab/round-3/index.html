Unjustified first. Four ways to spend the width.

One page, round 2's A2 with round 1 A's controls. The four differ only in where the text sits and what fills the spare width.

  1. LLeftA2 as it was: text on the left margin, spare width stays empty.
  2. CCentredThe column sits in the middle of the canvas.
  3. GGuttermalejandro.com's own grid. Lines, Σ, short and width live in the label gutter, right-aligned like WEB / ROLE / TIMELINE.
  4. WWide panelOn wide screens the panel takes the spare width and shows every group at once, in two columns.

From your notes

  • Unjustified alone by default. vs browser puts the native browser's column beside yours, and only then does the header show native browser → ragged / justified, to pick which of the browser's two it is. No A / B / A+B.
  • Auto width by default: the column follows the window, up to 70 ch (Auto up to, under Type's more). Dragging the slider or the handle fixes it; auto or a double-click on the handle brings it back.
  • Always editable. Click into the text and type. Comparing, both columns share one text.
  • read: only the text, on the site's content column, like a post. ↙ or Esc to come back.
  • Simple first. Each group shows its basics; n more ↓ opens the rest and says how many of those you've changed. It slides open (grid rows 0fr → 1fr, so Safari and Firefox animate it too).
  • Hyphens and Kashida show nothing while off, only their switch. start is the default now. Five ways to switch them, under box at the panel's foot. Off, the line beside the title says what the group does, so it reads as a sentence, not a hole. gutter: the box before the title. edge: the box after it, at the gutter's edge, pointing at the options. start: the box at the start of the options, with what it does after it. switch: off / on, as the header's left / centre. cell: one rectangle that presses, a square at its corner, from round 1 C. The title toggles in all five, and stays put as the options open.
  • Group titles at the same size as the labels beside them, set further off, and more room between rows.
  • Reset by gesture: double-click or double-tap a slider or its label. A tick on the track marks the default; the thumb covers it, so it shows only once you've moved away, and dragging near it snaps back.
  • Linked pairs keep their ratio, like a locked aspect ratio. Tracking starts ±0.012 em; word spacing's slider moves only the stretch (+200%); the shrink stays at −20% unless you split it. Unlinked, the row stays as a heading over two indented rows, Shrink and Stretch, so nothing jumps. Relinking keeps the ratio you set.
  • Strike toggles from specimen T-04 for auto and linked: off is struck through and faded, the strike in full ink. To read as buttons, not part of the label, they come in four looks under buttons at the panel's foot: plain as before, underline (the default; specimen S-11, as ch / %: in ink and underlined when on, half ink and struck through when off), outline (a box, struck when off; the default), fill (a box, black when on). Each reaches past its word, for fingers.
  • The header's switches, under header at the panel's foot: block, the site's black block, as before; stops, specimen S-11, the chosen word in ink and underlined, the rest at half ink, so vs browser / focus / read all rest at half while none is on; idle, the same, except a row with nothing on stays in ink, and its other words step back only once one is on.
  • Marks on every dropdown and every "n more", under marks at the panel's foot, since ↓ had become a pattern: arrow, as before; chevron, a drawn hairline V; caret, a small solid triangle; plus, + / − on "more", a chevron on dropdowns; corner, a dog-ear in the dropdown's corner, as a toolbar tool that hides more; none, no mark, the box says dropdown and "more" reads as a link; quiet (the default, your pick), none on dropdowns and a chevron on "more". The mark turns over as "more" opens.
  • Dropdowns with the title inside, under dropdown at the panel's foot: stack, the title above a box, as before; inline (the default), one box, title on the left and value on the right, as a slider's row; caption, a small title in the box's corner over the value; legend, the title set into the top border, as on a drawing's frame; split, a cell for the title ruled off from the value's, as a spec sheet; rule, no box, a line under the row, black on hover. The browser's own menu still opens, the phone's picker too.
  • Where a slider's option sits, under options at the panel's foot: label, beside the label, with room above the slider; value, beside the number, where ch / % becomes the number's own unit (6 ch / %); below, a line under the slider as Hyphens' start, a box, the word and what it does; pointer, value for a mouse and below for a finger; hybrid, auto and linked beside the label, where switching them never moves them, ch / % beside the number, every slider with the label's room above it; glide (the default), hybrid, and linked slides to the row's end as a pair splits, striking through on the way, and back as it relinks. Hybrid and glide go below for a finger too. On a touch screen the sliders are taller, the thumbs bigger, and the line below as tall as a fingertip.
  • Header: left / centre switch in place. focus fades everything but the text and itself, without moving anything (Esc too); read rebuilds the page around the text.
  • Kashida is a group of its own, laid out like Hyphens: on/off and per line, then frequency, per word, shortest word, and which letters stretch (after س ش ص ض, before a final ة ه د ذ or ا ل ك, any other joint).
  • Indent in ch or in % of the width (the ch / % beside it, set as specimen S-11's word stops: the chosen one in ink and underlined, the other at half ink; switching keeps the indent where it is). It never shows past 90% of the width: a narrower width holds a ch indent in (its number goes grey), and widening gives it back. For broken paragraphs. Size now goes to 4.5 rem and Line height is under Type's more, for the display-size version.
  • Units a developer would write: width and indent in ch, size in rem. Changing the typeface or size keeps the characters a line.
  • Paragraph numbers, faint, outside the text on its starting side.

Also new
