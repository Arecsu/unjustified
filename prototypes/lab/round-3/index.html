Unjustified first. Four ways to spend the width.

One page, round 2's A2 with round 1 A's controls. The four differ only in where the text sits and what fills the spare width.

  1. LLeftA2 as it was: text on the left margin, spare width stays empty.
  2. CCentredThe column sits in the middle of the canvas.
  3. GGuttermalejandro.com's own grid. Lines, Σ, short and width live in the label gutter, right-aligned like WEB / ROLE / TIMELINE.
  4. WWide panelOn wide screens the panel takes the spare width and shows every group at once, in two columns.

From your notes

  • Unjustified alone by default. Hold ragged or justified in the header to see the browser's own text in its place until you let go. side by side puts the browser's column beside yours, as whichever of the two you pressed last. No A / B / A+B.
  • Auto width by default: the column follows the window, up to 70 ch (Auto up to, under Type's more). Dragging the slider or the handle fixes it; auto or a double-click on the handle brings it back.
  • Always editable. Click into the text and type. Comparing, both columns share one text.
  • read: only the text, on the site's content column, like a post. ↙ or Esc to come back.
  • Simple first. Each group shows its basics; n more ↓ opens the rest and says how many of those you've changed.
  • Reset by gesture: double-click or double-tap a slider or its label. A tick on the track marks the default; the thumb covers it, so it shows only once you've moved away, and dragging near it snaps back.
  • Linked pairs keep their ratio, like a locked aspect ratio. Tracking starts ±0.012 em; word spacing's slider moves only the stretch (+200%); the shrink stays at −20% unless you split it. Unlinked, the row stays as a heading over two indented rows, Shrink and Stretch, so nothing jumps. Relinking keeps the ratio you set.
  • Strike toggles from specimen T-04 for auto and linked: off is struck through and faded, the strike in full ink.
  • Header: left / centre switch in place. focus fades everything but the text and itself, without moving anything (Esc too); read rebuilds the page around the text.
  • Kashida is a group of its own, laid out like Hyphens: on/off and per line, then frequency, per word, shortest word, and which letters stretch (after س ش ص ض, before a final ة ه د ذ or ا ل ك, any other joint).
  • Indent in ch, for broken paragraphs. Size now goes to 4.5 rem and Line height is under Type's more, for the display-size version.
  • Units a developer would write: width and indent in ch, size in rem. Changing the typeface or size keeps the characters a line.
  • Paragraph numbers, faint, outside the text on its starting side.

Also new

  • Under each column's head, a ruler in four styles (Show › Ruler): hairline, dimension, ticks or none. Its bar at the end is the rag zone, so you see Rag change as you drag it.
  • Alone, the head's label is yours to pick (Show › Label): the type spec, 19/29.5 Source Serif 4; the block's size; a figure caption; the sample; or nothing. Comparing, Unjustified and Browser.
  • The status bar counts what nothing else shows: characters a line, minutes to read, tatweels.
  • A faint width handle that darkens when you reach for it. No crop marks.
  • The canvas is framed like an app window: the block sits in its vertical middle (it holds still while you type), and a status bar along the bottom edge shows paragraphs, words, characters and the setup.
  • Arabic mirrors the whole canvas: columns anchor on the right, the handle moves to the left edge.

Real and stand-in

  • Really change the text: sample, direction, typeface, size, width, indent, line height, letter spacing, hyphenate, nearly justified, every kashida control, and Show's numbers, rag zone, guides and tatweels.
  • The unjustified column is still CSS (text-wrap: pretty), not the composer. Kashida is a rough stand-in: it stretches lines that have room, using the app's letter ranking.
  • Everything else only shows its value.
